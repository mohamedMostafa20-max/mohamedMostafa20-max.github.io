About

I am a civil and structural engineer specialized in steel structure design, with 200+ projects designed across the oil & gas, industrial, commercial and recreational sectors in Egypt, Saudi Arabia and the UAE, and hands-on experience in API 650 above-ground storage tanks. I design and check to AISC 360, ASCE 7, API 650, SBC and ECP, from concept and structural analysis through connection design, calculation notes and shop-drawing review.
