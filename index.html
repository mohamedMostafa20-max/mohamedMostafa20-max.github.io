Common questions

Which design codes do you work with?

AISC 360 and AISC 341, ASCE 7, API 650 for storage tanks, the Saudi Building Code (SBC 301 / 306) and the Egyptian codes (ECP 201 / 205).

Do you design projects outside Egypt?

Yes. I work remotely and have designed steel projects in Egypt, Saudi Arabia and the UAE.

What do I receive at the end of the design?

The structural calculation note, the design drawings and the connection details.

What do you need to price a steel structure design?

The architectural drawings, the location, the use of the building and its approximate area. Send them on WhatsApp or by email.

Which software do you use?

SAP2000, ETABS and STAAD.Pro for analysis and design, Dlubal RFEM 6 for tensile membranes, IDEA StatiCa for connections, and Tekla Structures, Revit and AutoCAD for models and drawings.

Are the online design tools really free?

Yes. They run in your browser with no install and no sign-up, and each one produces a step-by-step calculation report.
